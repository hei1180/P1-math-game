# P1 Maths curriculum reference (小一數學)

Working reference for designing games on this platform. Condensed from two EDB documents (PDFs kept locally in this folder, not committed):

- **KS1 explanatory notes** — 《小學數學課程闡釋（第一學習階段）》EDB 2018 (`EN_KS1_tc.pdf`). Source of every unit, learning point, time and boundary below (pages 7–21).
- **KLA curriculum guide** — 《數學教育學習領域課程指引（小一至中六）》CDC 2017 (`ME_KLACG_chi_2017_12_08.pdf`). Source of the aims, KS1 learning targets, KG→P1 transition, time allocation and generic-skill examples.

Code scheme: `1N2` = **1** (Primary 1) + **N** (strand) + **2** (unit number). Strands: **N** 數 Number · **M** 度量 Measures · **S** 圖形與空間 Shape & Space · **D** 數據處理 Data Handling (no D unit in P1; first is 2D1) · **F** 進階 Further applications. Numbered items under a unit are its 學習重點 (learning points), referred to here as `1N1.5` etc.

The order of units is **not** a teaching order; schools sequence them themselves.

## 1. Overall aims (課程宗旨, KLA guide §2.1)

Maths education develops students':

| | 中文 | English (gloss) |
|---|---|---|
| a | 明辨性思考、創意、構思、探究及數學推理；運用數學建立及解決問題 | critical thinking, creativity, inquiry, reasoning; posing and solving problems |
| b | 透過數學語言溝通，清晰及邏輯地表達 | communicating clearly and logically in mathematical language |
| c | 運用數字、符號及其他數學物件 | using numbers, symbols and other mathematical objects |
| d | 數字感、符號感、空間感、度量感、鑑辨結構和規律 | number sense, symbol sense, spatial sense, measure sense, seeing structure and pattern |
| e | 對數學學習持正面態度，欣賞數學的美學及文化 | positive attitude; appreciating the beauty and culture of maths |

## 2. KS1 learning targets (小一至小三的學習目標)

These span P1–P3; P1 covers the early part of each.

- **Number 數:** concepts of whole numbers (non-negative) and simple fractions; commutative and associative properties of + and ×; four operations on whole numbers, + and − of simple fractions, checking answers are reasonable; using numbers to pose and solve simple problems.
- **Measures 度量:** length, distance, weight, capacity; comparing by different methods and recording; why standard units are needed; choosing tools and units; estimating; money, time and dates in daily life; simple problems combining number, measures and shape.
- **Shape & Space 圖形與空間:** recognise and describe 2-D and 3-D shapes intuitively; points, lines, faces; right/acute/obtuse angles; perpendicular and parallel; square, rectangle, parallelogram, trapezium; inclusion relations of quadrilaterals and triangles; making 2-D shapes and appreciating geometric beauty; relative position and the four directions.
- **Data Handling 數據處理:** why data is organised; collecting and classifying; simple statistical charts; problems from data. *(Starts P2.)*

## 3. Teaching guidance relevant to P1 (KLA guide)

- **KG → P1 transition (§3.6.1):** pre-school children learn early maths (counting, comparing) through hands-on activities, games, stories and daily experience; the focus there is interest in maths. Lower primary should keep linking maths to children's experience and daily life, with varied activities such as handling real objects, measuring and drawing. P1 teachers should consolidate Number and Shape & Space concepts as the base for later learning. Let children handle and observe shapes and describe them in proper maths words (e.g. 「正方形」「直線」).
- **Time allocation (§3.8):** KS1 (P1–P3) = 12–15% of lesson time, 285–356 hours over three years. The P1 units below total about 95 suggested hours.
- **Generic skills at KS1 (Appendix 2, communication):** follow spoken instructions; read pictures, shapes and symbols (+ − =) and describe them in plain words ("2 加 3" for 2+3); express results with pictures and symbols; discuss in groups.
- **KS1 exemplars in the guide:** ① 手拉手 *Hand in hand* — odd/even by pairing classmates (Number; transition; communication, critical thinking, problem solving), ② 分物遊戲 *sharing game* (Number, P2 division), ③ 你的量杯 *your measuring cup* (Measures, project learning).

## 4. P1 learning units (from the KS1 explanatory notes)

Hours = suggested teaching time in the notes. **Boundaries** are what P1 students do *not* need yet; they matter for setting game difficulty.

### Number 數範疇

#### 1N1 20 以內的數 — Numbers to 20 · 13.5 h
1. 認識 1-20 各數 — read and write 1–20; count a set of ≤20 objects; take a given amount (≤20) from a pile
2. 進行順數和倒數 — count on and count back, **in 1s only** in this unit
3. 認識序數和基數的概念 — ordinal vs cardinal (第一/1 … 第二十/20); any numerals allowed incl. Chinese; students need not use the words 序數/基數
4. 認識 20 以內的奇數和偶數 — odd/even ≤20, via daily-life activities (e.g. pairing up to hold hands); at KS1 may be called 單數 / 雙數
5. 認識 2-18 各數的分解和合成 — compose two **single-digit** numbers into one number and split a number into **two single-digit numbers** (4 and 8 → 12; 12 → 4 and 8); hands-on first; apply it (12 dots, 8 covered → 4 seen); say the result aloud first, then record with words and + − =; learn the names 加號 減號 等號 (symbols here are for recording, not calculating). Teachers may say 「分為」 instead of 「分解為」.

Also in this unit: compare two sets (one-to-one matching or counting) and say 「甲比乙多」/「乙比甲少」; link to 5 比 3 大.
**Boundaries:** no > and < symbols in P1; no "A has 2 fewer than B" (that is 1N2).
**Vocabulary:** 比……多、比……少、比……大、比……小、順數、倒數、雙數、單數、第一/1…第二十/20、加、減、等於、加號、減號

#### 1N2 基本加法和減法 — Basic addition and subtraction · 13.5 h
1. 理解加法和減法的基本概念 — built on 分解和合成; + = combining/adding on; − = taking away/comparing; results are 和 and 差
2. 進行 18 以內的加法和減法口算 — mental + and − within 18
3. 認識 0 的概念 — 0 via take-away stories; 0 + n = n, n + 0 = n, n − 0 = n, n − n = 0
4. 理解加法和減法的關係 — related facts (2+3=5, 5−2=3, 5−3=2); numbers kept within 10 for this exploration
5. 認識加法交換性質 — 5+4 = 4+5; subtraction is not commutative (5−2 ≠ 2−5); the term need not be learnt

**Boundaries:** word problems are mainly **picture-based**; solve mentally, then record horizontally (橫式); only the single-digit combinations from 1N1 (so **not** 12+5 or 17−5); no vertical form.
**Vocabulary:** 共有、合計、取去、餘下、剩下、加法、減法、和、差、橫式

#### 1N3 100 以內的數 — Numbers to 100 · 6 h
1. 認識 21-100 各數 — count, read, write
2. 認識個位和十位的概念 — tens and ones (24: 2 in tens = 20, 4 in ones = 4); the term 位值 not needed
3. 比較數的大小 — order a group of numbers 由大至小 / 由小至大; > and < wait until 2N1
4. 以每 2 個、5 個和 10 個一組進行數數 — skip-count by 2, 5, 10 (≤100 objects, no calculation); later used for coins and clock minutes
5. 估計物件的數量 — estimate (≤100) using benchmarks, only after enough counting experience

Also: 0 is even (count back 8, 6, 4, 2, 0); odd/even of a 2-digit number from its ones digit.
**Vocabulary:** 個位、十位、最大、最小、估計

#### 1N4 加法和減法（一）— Addition and subtraction (I) · 13 h
1. 進行兩個數的加法運算 — add two numbers (up to 2 digits, **with carrying**)
2. 進行三個數的加法運算 — add three numbers, left to right (10+6+2 = 16+2 = 18)
3. 認識加法結合性質 — associative property to calculate faster (term not required)
4. 進行兩個數的減法運算 — subtract two numbers, **no borrowing**
5. 認識加法和減法的直式 — vertical form, line up tens and ones; may mark the carried digit
6. 解簡易應用題 — simple **word** problems, two numbers only; no written working or answer sentence

Also: check subtraction by addition.
**Boundaries:** sums < 100; three-number word problems are 2N2.
**Vocabulary:** 一位數、兩位數、對位、進位、直式

### Measures 度量範疇

#### 1M1 長度和距離（一）— Length and distance (I) · 3.5 h
1. 認識長度和距離的概念
2. 直觀比較 — compare by looking (use obviously different lengths)
3. 直接比較 — compare directly (line up one end)
4. 以自訂單位比較 — compare with self-chosen units; includes curved/bendable things (string)

Distance = straight-line length between two objects; tell distance from path length.
**Boundaries:** don't ask students to order many objects by length at once.
**Vocabulary:** 長度、距離、長、短、高、矮、闊、窄、厚、薄、遠、近

#### 1M2 貨幣（一）— Money (I) · 6 h
1. 認識香港流通的硬幣 — colour, shape, pictures, size, thickness, weight of HK coins
2. 認識價錢牌上標價的記法 — read price tags (「$2.50」= 二元五角), **≤ $10**; no decimal place value; students don't write price tags
3. 認識硬幣在日常生活中的應用 — count a set of coins worth **≤ $10** (no written calculation); exchange **within** {1角, 2角, 5角, $1} (5 個二角 = 1 個一元) and **within** {$1, $2, $5, $10} (1 個十元 = 1 個五元 + 2 個二元 + 1 個一元)

**Boundaries:** no exchange across the two groups (no $5 → 10 個五角); giving change is 2N5.
**Vocabulary:** 硬幣、元、角、買、賣、便宜、昂貴

#### 1M3 長度和距離（二）— Length and distance (II) · 4 h
1. 認識厘米 (cm) — why a standard unit is needed
2. 以厘米為單位量度及比較 — use a cm ruler (start at 0, read start and end marks); choose the right tool
3. 運用永備尺估計量度結果 — body benchmarks (thumb width, foot length, hand span); estimate before measuring

**Vocabulary:** 厘米 (cm)、厘米尺、軟尺、永備尺

#### 1M4 時間（一）— Time (I) · 6 h
1. 以「時」和「半時」報時 — read o'clock and half past on an analogue clock
2. 認識小時 (h) — feel how long an hour is
3. 以小時為單位量度及比較時間間隔 — whole hours, ≤ 12 h; faster/slower
4. 解有關時間間隔的簡單應用題 — only two types: start + interval → end; start and end → interval (whole hours ≤ 12)
5. 認識一星期有七天及各天的名稱 — no need to stress which day starts the week
6. 認識一年有 12 個月及各月份的名稱 — about 4 weeks a month
7. 認識月曆 — read dates and weekdays off a calendar

**Boundaries:** students don't draw clock hands; no "just after 9", "nearly 9", "about 9".
**Vocabulary:** ……時、……時半、時針、分針、小時、年、月、日、星期

### Shape & Space 圖形與空間範疇

#### 1S1 立體圖形（一）— 3-D shapes (I) · 6 h
1. 認識角柱、圓柱、角錐、圓錐和球的直觀概念 — prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone, sphere by touching, stacking, rolling and observing; link solids to their pictures (trace outlines on photos)

**Boundaries:** no tilted solids (oblique prism/cone); no making solids (5S2); no names of different prisms/pyramids (2S1); 「球」/「球體」 both fine.
**Vocabulary:** 立體圖形、角柱、角錐、圓柱、圓錐、球

#### 1S2 平面圖形 — 2-D shapes · 10 h
1. 認識點、直線和曲線的直觀概念 — a point has no size; one straight line through two points but many curves; lines have no thickness
2. 繪畫和製作直線和曲線 — trace edges of cups/books, use string
3. 認識三角形、四邊形、五邊形、六邊形和圓形的基本概念 — identify by counting sides (concave allowed, but no concave/convex terms)
4. 繪畫和製作這些圖形
5. 以這些圖形拼砌平面圖形 — free or set designs; a shape stays the same shape when turned; appreciate beauty

**Boundaries:** only simple shapes; line segments are 2S4.
**Vocabulary:** 點、直線、曲線、平面圖形、三角形、四邊形、五邊形、六邊形、圓形

#### 1S3 方向和位置（一）— Direction and position (I) · 3.5 h
1. 從觀測者的角度以「上」「下」「左」「右」「前」「後」「之間」描述物件的相對位置 — first-person activities, then pictures; left/right from the student's own left/right (「在左」「在右」); 「___在___的上面/下面/前面/後面」

**Boundaries:** keep it simple; no complex relative-position problems.
**Vocabulary:** 位置、上、下、左、右、前、後、之間

### Further applications 進階學習單位

#### 1F1 探索與研究 — Inquiry and investigation · 10 h
Discover and build knowledge through activities; improve inquiry, communication, thinking and concept forming. Not a stand-alone unit: run it inside other units (motivation, development, consolidation or assessment), including enrichment, cross-unit and cross-KLA activities.

## 5. Prior-knowledge links later units make to P1

From 「小一」 mentions in the KS1 notes. Useful for "bridge" levels at the top of a game.

- 2N1 builds on 1N3 (tens/ones, skip-counting by 2/5/10 → by 20/25/50/100; > < introduced).
- 2M3 Money (II) builds on 1M2 (coins known in P1).
- 3M2/3M4 Time builds on P1–P2 hours and minutes, 12-hour clock, and simple end-time / interval problems.
